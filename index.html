01 Prototype / Work in Progress

FloodGuard

Smart drainage monitoring for early flood prevention.

FloodGuard is a low-cost IoT-based smart drainage monitoring concept designed to detect probable drain blockages, monitor water levels, and identify flood-prone locations early through a live dashboard.

  • Inspired by urban flash-flooding and blocked-drain challenges
  • Built around sensor-based monitoring and location-based alerts
  • Shaped through field visits and discussions with stakeholders

02 Concept Project

SpaceTrash Hack

Sustainable waste management for future Mars missions.

A sustainable-system concept explored during NASA Space Apps / SpaceTrash Hack, focused on managing, reusing, recycling, and repurposing inorganic waste during long-duration human missions to Mars.

  • Focus on closed-loop resource management
  • Explores waste-to-resource approaches for space habitats
  • Developed through research, teamwork, and solution design
